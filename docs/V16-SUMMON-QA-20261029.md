They retained full health, 0 hits and 0 externally assigned invulnerability. Independent forecast and linear input policies both demonstrated five-cycle no-hit routes. Attack/skill automation was disabled to observe the complete cycles; these do not establish every seed is fair or that a human can reproduce the route.
